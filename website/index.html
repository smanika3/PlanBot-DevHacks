From one message to a booked plan.

  1. Exit 1

    Someone says it out loud

    PlanBot spots the plan and pins one card that edits itself. Each activity gets its own “I’m in” button.

    free this weekend, wanna golf? then dinner after
  2. Exit 2

    Friends opt in, per activity

    Join golf, dinner or both. Planning starts once the organizer and one more person are in.

  3. Exit 3

    Availability, in plain chat

    No calendar access. The bot repeats back what it understood so nobody is misread, then ranks times and checks real Tempe venues, with opening hours read live.

    free Saturday 9am to 6pm, budget under $$$
  4. Exit 4

    Needs stay private

    Diet goes by DM. The card shows “Dietary: vegetarian”, never who. A hard need narrows dinner to venues whose own site confirms it.

  5. Exit 5

    Clashes go to the group

    When a time conflicts, that person is marked tentatively out. The group chooses: go without them, or find a time for everyone. It never says why someone is busy.

  6. Exit 6

    Vote, then lock

    Approve every option you can do. Ties get one re-vote, then the organizer decides. Each activity locks once everyone in it has voted.

  7. Exit 7

    One person books

    PlanBot never books or pays. A volunteer gets the venue, link, time and party size by DM, and confirms when it is done.

What it will not do.

  • Make up opening hours. If it cannot confirm them, the option says “hours unverified”.
  • Reveal who needs what. The group sees needs, not names, and never a reason for “busy”.
  • Book or pay for anyone. A human always confirms the booking.

Next exit: your server.
