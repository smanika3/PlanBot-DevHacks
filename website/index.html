PlanBot spots the plan and pins one card that edits itself, with an “I’m in” button per activity.

Friends opt in, per activity.

Join golf, dinner or both. Planning starts once the organizer and one more person are in.

Availability, in plain chat.

No calendar access. The bot repeats back what it understood, then ranks times and checks real Tempe venues, with opening hours read live.

Needs stay private.

Diet goes by DM. The card shows the need, never who. A hard need narrows dinner to venues whose own site confirms it.

Clashes go to the group.

A conflicting time marks that person tentatively out. The group picks: go without them, or find a time for everyone. It never says why.

Vote, then lock.

Approve every option you can do. Ties get one re-vote, then the organizer decides. Each activity locks once everyone in it has voted.

One person books.

PlanBot never books or pays. A volunteer gets the venue, link, time and party size by DM, and confirms when it is done.

What it will not do.

  • Make up opening hours. If it cannot confirm them, the option says “hours unverified”.
  • Reveal who needs what. The group sees needs, not names, and never a reason for “busy”.
  • Book or pay for anyone. A human always confirms the booking.
